User Flow Architecture • PDF Slides 2 & 5

A Transparent, Step-by-Step Service Journey

From initial onboarding to formal deed notarization, every milestone is designed without bureaucratic friction.

01

Home Dashboard & Needs Discovery

Users are welcomed by an intuitive interface featuring instant search and four primary hubs: Templates, Notary Chat, Business Account, and Video Consultation.

02

Licensed Notary & Specialist Selection

Comprehensive directory of certified Notaries & PPAT with verified legal specialties, office locations, client ratings, and transparent rates.

03

Interactive & Video Consultation Room

Direct confidential dialogue with the assigned notary. Notaries can attach official checklists or initiate an encrypted live video session.

04

Template Access & Contract Customization

Catalog of ready-to-use legal drafts. Users can review essential clauses, download documents, or request notary verification.

05

Interactive Guides & Business Checklist

Real-time progress tracker for establishing business entities (PT/CV), complete with interactive checklist toggles and deadline reminders.

06

Integrated Secure Payment Gateway

Transparent checkout supporting Virtual Accounts from major national banks, popular e-wallets, standardized QRIS, and credit cards.

07

Corporate Dashboard & B2B Retainer

Centralized corporate portal for businesses requiring continuous advisory, periodic OSS compliance, and digital notarial deed archives.
